## Details worth preserving

The [strategy page](https://cadre.ai/strategy) describes eight transformation areas: a dedicated team, an organization-wide AI platform, culture and adoption, connected systems, data readiness, agent readiness, departmental analysis, and a longer-term vision. These can explain the framework if needed. They do not supply the assessment's scoring method. The page also advertises a 45-day transformation intensive; do not reuse that duration for maturity scoring, all projects, or delivery commitments.

The [engineering page](https://cadre.ai/ai-engineering) supports concrete examples such as document routing, email triage, reporting, customer inquiries, and API connections. These can enrich service answers without implying account access. Its examples of software subscription amounts are not Cadre service prices; retain the pricing redirection.

The [leadership page](https://cadre.ai/leadership-facilitation) describes experiential executive/team sessions and several session formats. Those are optional future facts within the existing overview topic, not evidence of available appointment slots or contract terms.

The [privacy policy](https://cadre.ai/legal/privacy-policy) was labeled updated **09/01/2026**. It covers personal information associated with the website and services, states general safeguards, and expressly disclaims complete security. Its existence corrects an omission from the current knowledge set, whose wording narrowly says policies are not in the assistant's knowledge. Do not map the policy automatically onto this separately hosted chatbot, a specific model provider, or a client's contracted deployment. A policy document is not a certification or an audit report.

The [strategy page](https://cadre.ai/strategy) calls Cadre an official OpenAI service partner. Treat that as a Cadre-published claim. The existing configuration cites the supplied company profile for a broader platform/partner list. Generic logo labels in the extracted site text are insufficient to elevate all those relationships to publicly verified formal partnerships.
