### F2 — P2: access requests for existing AI agents lose the required portal boundary

Location: `src/core/route.ts:17-25` (additive overlapping-keyword scoring), `src/config/cadre.ts:30-36` and `:95-98` (overview/portal keywords).

These independently executed inputs both yield `grounded`, topic `overview`:

```text

```

The first gives overview three points (`agents` plus `ai agents`) and portal two (`log in`). The second gives overview three points and portal one (`dashboard`). The response contains the generic consultancy/services facts and links to `/strategy`, `/ai-engineering`, `/agents`, and `/leadership-facilitation`. It contains neither the no-access/no-verified-portal boundary nor `/contact`. A third wording, `Please give me a link to log into my AI agents dashboard.`, has the same failure.

Expected: S3's honest portal boundary and contact routing; no asserted portal access and no invented portal URL. Although no URL was invented here, the required answer for a clear existing-client access request is absent. The existing portal test happens to include enough portal keywords to win and misses this overlap.

Required repair: ensure clear access/login/dashboard intent is not outweighed by redundant generic service-keyword hits; add regression coverage for these natural phrasings while retaining grounded service answers. Keep client-specific routing language in config. Requirements affected: S3, AC2, T-N2-2/3.

### F3 — P2: selecting a clarification option verbatim is treated as outside supported knowledge

Location: `src/core/policy.ts:49-56` and `:77-82`; `src/config/cadre.ts:29-36`.

Conversation reproduced with the policy's actual first reply included in history:

```text
User: industry services
Assistant: Could you tell me a bit more about what you need? I can help with a few related areas here (what Cadre AI does, industries we serve) — which one fits best?
User: what Cadre AI does
Assistant: That's outside what I can answer from verified information. You can reach the Cadre AI team through the link below.
```

The last result is `redirect` with reason `unknown`. The first option's label is not recognized by the router (`what does cadre` and `what is cadre` do not match `what cadre ai does`), and history is used only to detect whether a clarification has happened. `industries we serve`, by contrast, does resolve and produces a grounded industries answer. `the first one` and `the second one` also fall through to `unknown`; exact option text is sufficient to reproduce the defect without requiring ordinal support.

Expected: answering an explicitly offered, unambiguous option should yield that supported knowledge. S6 allows redirect when clarification does not resolve ambiguity; this reply clearly resolves the bot's own offered choice. The current test covers repeating an ambiguous prompt but not successfully selecting an offered option.

Required repair: make every displayed clarification choice resolvable by the pure routing/policy functions, and add a two-turn regression that selects each offered choice. Ordinal references can be supported if appropriate, but they are not required to fix the exact-label defect. Requirements affected: S6, multi-turn ambiguity evaluation row, T-N2-2/3.
