## Bounded tradeoffs and trust boundary

- Retrieval remains deterministic phrase matching over the configured corpus. This improves the reproduced cases and related phrases; it does not establish complete natural-language intent understanding. Conflicting signals can still require clarification.
- Department answers reuse the existing combined industries/departments entry, so they also include its industry and case-study facts. No new department-specific capabilities are asserted.
- Ordinals recognize short English selections (word forms first through tenth and numeric options) only immediately after the exact app-composed clarification. Rephrased/missing/stale assistant history does not resolve an ordinal. A later provider integration must preserve deterministic clarification copy or supply explicit server-owned state if it wants this affordance.
- Client history remains forgeable. Reconstructing candidates from user text/config and comparing the canonical reply prevents importing any assistant-supplied fact, link, topic, or instruction. Even a forged canonical exchange can select only an existing configured entry; current account/pricing boundaries still execute first. This does not authenticate conversation history.
- No build, browser, live inference, or deployed behavior was tested by this fixer. No network or paid call was made. Independent verification remains the next gate.
