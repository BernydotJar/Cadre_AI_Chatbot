# Owner authorization — Donna premium experience

Date: 2026-09-09 UTC.

The owner explicitly requested a stricter premium UI/UX pass after productization. The target is a high-taste Silicon Valley / enterprise product presentation: large editorial typography, restrained composition, generous negative space, minimal navigation, the chatbot presented as a refined tool, and cinematic visual presence without becoming decorative noise.

## Authorized increment

- Re-review the current public Cadre site and the deployed Donna experience before changing the design.
- Use a strict independent UI/UX critic before release; retain negative findings and repairs as evidence.
- Refine Donna's visual identity, avatar, and small reusable icon/motion language while preserving profile-driven composition.
- Treat the supplied geometric neon references as **motion/composition references only**, not as a license to copy their exact art, palette, or composition.
- Add an optional ambient media layer designed for a muted 6–10 second loop with a static poster, low visual aggression, bounded payload, and `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback.
- Keep the primary experience accessible, responsive, fast, and useful with or without motion.
- Continue using the existing Vercel project and existing safe provider/runtime boundaries.

## Design boundary

The premium pass must increase perceived quality by reduction, hierarchy, spacing, typography, and controlled motion—not by adding dashboard chrome, fake metrics, stock-office imagery, cyberpunk clutter, autonomous-agent theatrics, or unsupported product claims. The Cadre visual system remains the authority for brand fit; the candidate experience may be more cinematic, but it must still read as Cadre-adjacent rather than a separate neon AI brand.

No new database, auth, CRM, analytics, paid media service, vector store, GraphRAG, arbitrary page scraping, real email delivery, or new external action is authorized by this increment.
